Overview

This website supports a thesis on the lived experiences of people with depersonalisation-derealisation disorder (DPDR). It analyses publicly available posts from the subreddit r/dpdr using topic modelling (BERTopic), and sentiment analysis and emotion analysis (RoBERTa).

For privacy reasons, the raw Reddit data is not included in the supplement materials.

Ethical data handling

The analysis used publicly available Reddit posts from r/dpdr. To ensure that users cannot be identified, raw Reddit data and quotes are not shared.

Ethical approval: Ethical approval for the study was obtained from the Humanities & Social Sciences Ethics Committee of the University of Twente (Application nr.: 252486).

Data availability. The materials here let you inspect the workflow and aggregated results. They do not allow individual posts to be reconstructed.
